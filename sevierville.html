Area guide

Sevierville

Sevierville, nestled on the edge of the Great Smoky Mountains, offers a delightful mix of outdoor beauty and hometown charm. Known for its picturesque landscapes, historical sites, and family attractions, Sevierville is an ideal destination for those who appreciate both nature and relaxed, small-town vibes. It's a gateway to outdoor adventures and a haven for relaxation.

Landmarks and attractions

Popular places to see and do in and around Sevierville.

Douglas Lake

Douglas Lake, nestled near the Smoky Mountains, is a peaceful haven for nature lovers and outdoor enthusiasts. This picturesque lake offers a tranquil escape with activities like boating, fishing, and swimming, surrounded by stunning mountain vistas. It's the perfect spot for those seeking a quiet retreat or an active day in the great outdoors. Douglas Lake is not just a destination; it's an experience that embodies the serene beauty and recreational joy of the Tennessee outdoors.

Visit Douglas Lake

Rainforest Zoo

The RainForest Adventures Discovery Zoo in the Smoky Mountains is a hidden gem where visitors can get up close with the wonders of the animal kingdom. This unique zoo features a diverse collection of exotic creatures from rainforests around the world, offering an educational and exciting experience for all ages. From playful monkeys to colorful parrots, the zoo provides a captivating glimpse into the vibrant life of the tropics. It's an enchanting escape that brings the magic of the rainforest to life, right in the heart of Tennessee.

Visit Rainforest Zoo

Deer Farm

The Deer Farm Exotic Petting Zoo and Riding Stables in the Smoky Mountains offers a delightful outdoor experience unlike any other. Here, visitors can interact up-close with friendly deer and a variety of other animals, creating unforgettable moments for all ages. The farm also offers horseback riding, allowing guests to explore the scenic beauty of the area in a charming and leisurely way. It's not just a visit to a zoo; it's an opportunity to connect with nature and enjoy the simple pleasures of animal companionship and outdoor exploration.

Visit Deer Farm

Foxfire Park

Foxfire Mountain Adventure Park in the Smoky Mountains is a haven for those seeking both tranquility and thrill. This park offers a wide array of activities, from serene waterfall hikes to exhilarating zipline courses that soar above the lush landscape. Visitors can also test their courage on the swinging bridge or unwind by the creekside. It's more than just an adventure park; Foxfire Mountain is a place where natural beauty meets excitement, providing a perfect blend of relaxation and adventure for every visitor.

Visit Foxfire Park

Wahoo Ziplines

Wahoo Ziplines offers an unforgettable aerial adventure in the heart of the Smoky Mountains. As one of the area's premier zipline experiences, it provides a unique way to enjoy the breathtaking scenery from a thrilling new perspective. Glide over treetops and experience the exhilaration of flying, with views that stretch for miles. Perfect for thrill-seekers and nature enthusiasts alike, Wahoo Ziplines is more than just an activity; it's an exhilarating journey through the beauty of the Smokies, creating memories that last a lifetime.

Visit Wahoo Ziplines

Ninja Warrior Park

Ninja Warrior Park in the Smoky Mountains offers an exciting challenge for those looking to test their physical abilities and agility. Inspired by the popular Ninja Warrior TV series, this park features a variety of obstacle courses designed for all skill levels, from beginners to seasoned athletes. It's a place where fun meets fitness, as visitors push their limits, climb, jump, and swing through unique and challenging setups. Ninja Warrior Park isn't just a physical adventure; it's an opportunity to build confidence, strength, and have a blast while doing it.

Visit Ninja Warrior Park

Sevierville Golf Club

The Sevierville Golf Club offers a top-tier golfing experience set against the backdrop of the stunning Smoky Mountains. Boasting beautifully maintained courses that cater to players of all skill levels, this club is a golfer's paradise. Whether you're looking to challenge your game or simply enjoy a leisurely round in a picturesque setting, the Sevierville Golf Club provides the perfect blend of scenic beauty and golfing excellence. It's more than just a round of golf; it's an experience that combines sport, relaxation, and the natural splendor of Tennessee.

Visit Sevierville Golf Club

Forbidden Caverns

Step into the mesmerizing world of Forbidden Caverns in the Smoky Mountains, where you can uncover the hidden underground marvels of Tennessee. This natural attraction offers guided tours through a network of caves, showcasing stunning formations, sparkling streams, and a rich history. It's an educational and awe-inspiring journey, perfect for families and anyone curious about the mysteries beneath the earth's surface. Forbidden Caverns isn't just a cave tour; it's an exploration of natural beauty and geological wonders waiting to be discovered.

Visit Forbidden Caverns

Sevierville fun facts

Shoreline

Douglas has 555-miles of shoreline, a total surface area of 30,600-acres, and sees more than 1.7 million visitors per year

Fishing

Douglas Lake has been rated in the top 5 Crappie Fishing Lakes in the nation and in the top 10 Largemouth Bass Fishing Lakes.

Golf Course

Sevierville Golf Club was recently recognized by Golf Advisor as one of top 50 US Courses. The Sevierville Golf Club has now opened two new championship courses and features the River- a par 72 laid out along the (East prong or just the Little Pigeon River) and the Highland- a par 70 that winds through the lush hillsides. Both are beautiful, demanding and affordable

Dolly Parton

Dolly Parton was born and raised in Sevierville and a replica of her childhood cabin can be viewed by visitors at Dollywood

Festivals

Local festivals run year-round to keep the community coming together for tasty food, wonderful music,  and great times all around

Courthouse

Sevierville’s courthouse, now over 100 years old, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places and stands proud in the heart of downtown Sevierville.  Built in 1895-96, the courthouse is an excellent example of Victorian architecture.

Forbidden Caverns

During the early twenties and until 1943, a cave system called Forbidden Caverns was used to make moonshine. The constant water supply and the isolated location was ideal for Moonshiner’s to make their homemade whiskey.  Today, Forbidden Caverns is open for guided tours that explain the rich history of this cavern.
