Laurel Loft

Kalmia latifolia

Starting at$325,000
The Laurel Loft model was inspired by the Kalmia latifolia (Mountain Laurel), found throughout the Smoky Mountains. This 384 sq ft, 1 bed, 1 bath home includes a 16’ x 24’ footprint and a cozy 8’10” x 16’ loft.
A bright, modern design with a touch of rustic charm makes this lofted home both functional and fun.
A covered 8’ x 24’ rear deck and 8’ x 6’ entry add 240 sq ft of outdoor living space to enjoy.

Property Features

Finishes & Flexibility

Whether you're building a vacation rental or a personal retreat, the Laurel Loft model offers flexibility, comfort, and style. Buyers can personalize a wide range of finishes—including flooring, paint colors, cabinetry, lighting, hardware, and exterior siding—to match their vision. Want to add a larger deck, built-in shelves, or other custom touches? We're happy to help bring those ideas to life.

Systems & Comfort

The Laurel Loft model includes full residential systems installed by licensed professionals and built to code. A ductless mini split provides energy-efficient heating and cooling, while the electric fireplace adds ambiance. Standard residential electrical systems are included, plus a 220V line to power a hot tub on the deck. Water and sewer connections depend on the location—public utility or private well/septic. Insulation is up to code for year-round efficiency.

Foundation Options

Each Laurel Loft model can be built on either a block foundation or our treehouse-style elevated pier foundation. While some buyers choose based on style or budget, others may need elevation for steeper terrain. Both options are structurally sound, built to code, and chosen based on your land’s slope and soil. We'll help you choose the best foundation to suit your needs while ensuring long-term durability.

Land Included

No need to bring your own lot—the land is included in the purchase price. We handle the land search and purchase, presenting you with only carefully vetted properties that meet your goals. You'll have full approval over the final selection before we begin building. From Smoky Mountain views to wooded privacy, we’ll help you choose the best possible setting for your home.

Spacious Loft

The Laurel Loft adds functional square footage with a roomy sleeping loft—perfect for guests, kids, or even extra storage. Unlike many tiny home lofts that feel cramped, this space offers a ceiling height just over four feet in the center, providing surprising comfort and accessibility. Whether it’s used as a cozy reading nook or a bonus sleeping area, this loft transforms vertical space into real livable room—without compromising the footprint below.

Contemporary Edge

Laurel Loft offers the timeless appeal of a Smoky Mountain cabin with a clean, modern twist. Wood-toned finishes, gable rooflines, and warm textures give it that unmistakable cabin charm, while the contemporary layout and finishes keep it feeling fresh and updated. It’s the perfect balance for buyers who want rustic warmth with a touch of refinement—ideal for attracting renters or enjoying as your own elevated getaway.
