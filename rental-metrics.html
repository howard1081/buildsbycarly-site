Market notes

Sevierville notes

  • Short-break stays (3–4 nights) dominate; keep min-stay flexible on non-peak weeks.
  • Photo-forward design + hot tub + easy self-check-in lift ADR and reviews.

Pigeon Forge notes

  • Bundle ideas: show + dining partnerships to nudge mid-week conversions.
  • Keep calendar open for last-minute bookings; price smart 10–14 days out.

Gatlinburg notes

  • Prioritize view/privacy and outdoor space (deck, seating, lighting).
  • Invest in listing visuals; small upgrades here outperform discounting.
