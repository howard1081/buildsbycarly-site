About the Azalea

The Azalea model was named after Rhododendron calendulaceum, the vibrant Flame Azalea found throughout the Great Smoky Mountains. This 1 bed, 1 bath home features 575 sq ft of living space within a 19’2” x 30’ footprint.
Its thoughtful layout and open interior make it a comfortable and spacious retreat in any setting.
Outdoor space includes a covered 8’ x 18’2” rear deck and 4’ x 18’2” front porch — offering 230 sq ft of additional living area.

What sets the Azalea apart

Scandinavian-Inspired Design

The Azalea features a striking Scandinavian-style design—known for its clean lines, natural textures, and minimal-yet-warm aesthetic. This architectural style is especially popular for modern mountain getaways, offering a bright and airy feel inside and out. The open-concept layout, large windows, and sleek profile allow the home to feel expansive while maintaining a small footprint. Whether used as a rental or personal retreat, the Azalea blends modern simplicity with cozy charm.

Rear-Facing Window Wall

A standout feature of the Azalea is its rear-facing window wall—an architectural highlight that floods the interior with natural light. Perfectly positioned to showcase the mountain setting, this window wall creates a seamless connection between indoor and outdoor living. Whether you're watching a sunrise from the couch or framing sunset views from the dining nook, the Azalea's rear glass façade brings the outdoors in and makes the entire space shine.

Construction details

Finishes & Flexibility

Whether you're building a vacation rental or a personal retreat, the Azalea model offers flexibility, comfort, and style. Buyers can personalize a wide range of finishes—including flooring, paint colors, cabinetry, lighting, hardware, and exterior siding—to match their vision. Want to add a larger deck, built-in shelves, or other custom touches? We're happy to help bring those ideas to life.

Systems & Comfort

The Azalea model includes full residential systems installed by licensed professionals and built to code. A ductless mini split provides energy-efficient heating and cooling, while the electric fireplace adds ambiance. Standard residential electrical systems are included, plus a 220V line to power a hot tub on the deck. Water and sewer connections depend on the location—public utility or private well/septic. Insulation is up to code for year-round efficiency.

Foundation Options

Each Azalea model can be built on either a block foundation or our treehouse-style elevated pier foundation. While some buyers choose based on style or budget, others may need elevation for steeper terrain. Both options are structurally sound, built to code, and chosen based on your land’s slope and soil. We'll help you choose the best foundation to suit your needs while ensuring long-term durability.

Land Included

No need to bring your own lot—the land is included in the purchase price. We handle the land search and purchase, presenting you with only carefully vetted properties that meet your goals. You'll have full approval over the final selection before we begin building. From Smoky Mountain views to wooded privacy, we’ll help you choose the best possible setting for your home.
