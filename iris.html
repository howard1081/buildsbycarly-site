Welcome to

Iris

The Iris model was named after Iris versicolor, the vibrant Tennessee state flower found throughout the Smoky Mountains. This 1 bed, 1 bath A-frame offers 602 sq ft of main floor living and a 123 sq ft loft, all within a 21’6” x 28’ footprint.
From the loft, a covered balcony offers a quiet perch above the rear deck—perfect for morning coffee or stargazing in seclusion.
Outdoor living totals 415 sq ft and includes a 10’ x 21’6” covered rear deck, 4’ x 21’6” front porch, and the loft balcony.

PROPERTY FEATURES

Finishes & Flexibility

Whether you're building a vacation rental or a personal retreat, the Iris model offers flexibility, comfort, and style. Buyers can personalize a wide range of finishes—including flooring, paint colors, cabinetry, lighting, hardware, and exterior siding—to match their vision. Want to add a larger deck, built-in shelves, or other custom touches? We're happy to help bring those ideas to life.

Systems & Comfort

The Iris model includes full residential systems installed by licensed professionals and built to code. A ductless mini split provides energy-efficient heating and cooling, while the electric fireplace adds ambiance. Standard residential electrical systems are included, plus a 220V line to power a hot tub on the deck. Water and sewer connections depend on the location—public utility or private well/septic. Insulation is up to code for year-round efficiency.

Foundation Options

Each Iris model can be built on either a block foundation or our treehouse-style elevated pier foundation. While some buyers choose based on style or budget, others may need elevation for steeper terrain. Both options are structurally sound, built to code, and chosen based on your land’s slope and soil. We'll help you choose the best foundation to suit your needs while ensuring long-term durability.

Land Included

No need to bring your own lot—the land is included in the purchase price. We handle the land search and purchase, presenting you with only carefully vetted properties that meet your goals. You'll have full approval over the final selection before we begin building. From Smoky Mountain views to wooded privacy, we’ll help you choose the best possible setting for your home.

Compact A-Frame

Unlike most tiny homes of its size, the Iris is a true A-frame—an architectural feat that’s rarely achieved on a 330-square-foot footprint. Its sharply pitched roofline and clean, geometric design make it both iconic and efficient. With soaring ceilings and natural light pouring through the window wall, the Iris offers the dramatic charm of a full-size A-frame while preserving the scale and functionality needed for a small-footprint build.

Space-Saving

The Iris' bathroom door can be built as either a pocket door or a barn-style sliding door—both perfect for small-space living. These options eliminate the need for door swing clearance, giving you more usable room in the living area and bathroom alike. Whether you prefer the modern simplicity of a pocket door or the rustic charm of a barn door, this thoughtful detail makes everyday movement more efficient and stylish.

Elevated Cabin Style

The Juniper brings the timeless charm of a Smoky Mountain cabin into a refined, small-footprint design. Its clean lines, cozy proportions, and warm finishes reflect the rustic feel of a traditional cabin, while upgraded materials and attention to detail elevate it beyond the ordinary. Whether you're styling it with vintage touches or a modern rustic palette, the Juniper’s architecture is designed to blend seamlessly with natural surroundings—delivering comfort, character, and curb appeal in a home that’s as inviting as it is investment-ready.

Contemporary Cabin Fusion

While the Juniper leans into classic cabin style, it offers a fresh, updated take that blends mountain tradition with modern living. You’ll find nods to traditional design in its roofline, porch, and material palette—but with a cleaner, more contemporary edge that avoids heavy log cabin clichés. This fusion of styles makes the Juniper ideal for those who want the nostalgic coziness of a cabin with the lighter finishes and smarter layouts today’s buyers love. It’s the best of both worlds—elevated, but never overdone.
