Why Tiny Homes

Tiny homes and their enticing return on investment potential have piqued global interest with a wave of popularity surrounding their niche appeal and compact size and affordability.

These fully functional living spaces are typically approximately 500 square feet but have all the modern conveniences desirable to renters and owners alike. These popular abodes are filled with tons of positive appeal with one key element being the simplicity of maintaining them. With their smaller footprint, there are fewer repairs to be concerned with and maintenance requirements are minimal for an investor or owner.

For investors, the Great Smoky Mountains and surrounding areas offer an optimal opportunity to tap into the popularity and appeal of renting a tiny home for a calming mountain getaway. As a relatively new and niche concept, tiny homes continue to draw the interest of people more interested in a cozy experience versus vacationing in the typical oversized cabins which usually cost more money while keeping them apart.  

Despite their smaller size, tiny homes offer the same comforts and functionality as traditional homes, with an emphasis on efficient use of space. Not only are homes with smaller square footage more financially efficient for heating/cooling costs, but they also require less energy and gas to operate. In fact, each feature in a tiny home real estate project is meticulously considered for an exceptional outcome for both the user and the investor.
