Why Builds by Carly

Site-built construction

These are site-built tiny homes —not sheds, park models, or mobile units. Built on permanent foundations with licensed trades and code-aligned construction, our homes are designed to appraise like traditional builds, unlock better financing paths, and hold long-term value.

About our builds

Foundation options

We build on block and elevated pier foundations, including our signature treehouse-style pier system for steep or rocky terrain. It’s engineered for stability while creating the elevated views and “wow factor” that drives clicks, bookings, and higher nightly rates. Investor win: better sites become buildable—and more marketable.

Land selection support

Don’t already have land? We can help you identify and acquire the right property for your build and your rental goals. You approve the final selection—we guide you toward lots that fit your budget, access, slope, utilities, and the guest experience you want to sell.
