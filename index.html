WORKING WITH

CARLY BOWLEN

Builds by Carly is more than a name — it’s a team of professionals dedicated to crafting investment properties that merge beauty, quality, and performance. What began as a single land developer's vision has evolved into a trusted brand in the Smoky Mountains, known for site-built tiny homes that deliver big returns.
Our team understands what modern investors are looking for: properties that are visually striking, guest-ready, and built to last. From foundational engineering to design-forward finishes, we’ve thought through every square foot — because luxury lives in the details.
Founded by Carly Bowlen, a land developer with deep roots in Sevier County and a passion for innovation in the short-term rental market, Builds by Carly continues to set the standard for tiny homes with outsized impact.
Whether you're investing in your first property or expanding your portfolio, our site-built tiny homes offer an elevated entry point into the world of real estate — without compromising on comfort, craftsmanship, or return on investment.
