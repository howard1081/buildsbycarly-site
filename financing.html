Price terms

Additional terms: $15,000 construction deposit is nonrefundable and will be credited towards the final property purchase. Deposit is required on every Builds by Carly purchase and will be due upon signing the new construction real estate purchase and sales agreement. This commitment ensures both that our investors are equally dedicated to their new investment, and their readiness to move forward in securing their next short-term rental property.

Please note: Land is included in our pricing; however, certain upgrades, or desired property features such as mountain views, water access, or unique terrain-may affect the final cost. All pricing will be reviewed and confirmed before moving forward.
