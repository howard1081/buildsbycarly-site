About the Juniper

The Juniper is named after Juniperus virginiana, the native red cedar found throughout the Great Smoky Mountains. This 384 sq ft, 1 bed, 1 bath home is a cozy mountain retreat with a 16’ x 24’ footprint.
Thoughtfully designed for comfort and charm, it’s perfect for short-term stays or personal getaways.
An 8’ x 24’ outdoor deck and 8’ x 6’ roofed entrance provide 240 sq ft of additional outdoor living.

What sets the Juniper apart

Elevated Cabin Style

The Juniper brings the timeless charm of a Smoky Mountain cabin into a refined, small-footprint design. Its clean lines, cozy proportions, and warm finishes reflect the rustic feel of a traditional cabin, while upgraded materials and attention to detail elevate it beyond the ordinary. Whether you're styling it with vintage touches or a modern rustic palette, the Juniper’s architecture is designed to blend seamlessly with natural surroundings—delivering comfort, character, and curb appeal in a home that’s as inviting as it is investment-ready.

Contemporary Cabin Fusion

While the Juniper leans into classic cabin style, it offers a fresh, updated take that blends mountain tradition with modern living. You’ll find nods to traditional design in its roofline, porch, and material palette—but with a cleaner, more contemporary edge that avoids heavy log cabin clichés. This fusion of styles makes the Juniper ideal for those who want the nostalgic coziness of a cabin with the lighter finishes and smarter layouts today’s buyers love. It’s the best of both worlds—elevated, but never overdone.

Construction details

Finishes & Flexibility

Whether you're building a vacation rental or a personal retreat, the Juniper model offers flexibility, comfort, and style. Buyers can personalize a wide range of finishes—including flooring, paint colors, cabinetry, lighting, hardware, and exterior siding—to match their vision. Want to add a larger deck, built-in shelves, or other custom touches? We're happy to help bring those ideas to life.

Systems & Comfort

The Juniper model includes full residential systems installed by licensed professionals and built to code. A ductless mini split provides energy-efficient heating and cooling, while the electric fireplace adds ambiance. Standard residential electrical systems are included, plus a 220V line to power a hot tub on the deck. Water and sewer connections depend on the location—public utility or private well/septic. Insulation is up to code for year-round efficiency.

Foundation Options

Each Juniper model can be built on either a block foundation or our treehouse-style elevated pier foundation. While some buyers choose based on style or budget, others may need elevation for steeper terrain. Both options are structurally sound, built to code, and chosen based on your land’s slope and soil. We'll help you choose the best foundation to suit your needs while ensuring long-term durability.

Land Included

No need to bring your own lot—the land is included in the purchase price. We handle the land search and purchase, presenting you with only carefully vetted properties that meet your goals. You'll have full approval over the final selection before we begin building. From Smoky Mountain views to wooded privacy, we’ll help you choose the best possible setting for your home.
