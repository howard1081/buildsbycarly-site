Pigeon Forge

Sevier County, Tennessee

Pigeon Forge is a vibrant town bursting with family-friendly attractions, live shows, and unique experiences. Famous for Dollywood, the Island, and its array of amusement offerings, Pigeon Forge is the perfect blend of excitement and Southern charm. Whether seeking thrilling rides or delightful entertainment, visitors find endless enjoyment in this lively mountain town.

Landmarks and attractions

Landmark 01 of 07

Dollywood

Dollywood, nestled in the heart of the Smoky Mountains, is a vibrant blend of thrilling rides, traditional crafts, and live entertainment. This famed theme park, co-owned by Dolly Parton, offers a unique taste of Tennessee culture alongside its exciting attractions. Visitors can enjoy everything from roller coasters to authentic mountain music, making it a perfect destination for families and adventure seekers alike. Dollywood isn't just a park; it's a celebration of the rich heritage and spirited fun of the Smokies.

Visit Dollywood
Landmark 02 of 07

The Island

The Island in Pigeon Forge is a vibrant oasis of fun and relaxation, offering something for everyone. This lively entertainment complex captivates visitors with its unique mix of shops, restaurants, and amusement rides, including the iconic Great Smoky Mountain Wheel. Guests can enjoy leisurely strolls around the fountains, indulge in diverse culinary delights, or simply soak in the lively atmosphere. The Island is more than a destination; it's an experience where enjoyment and memorable moments are around every corner.

Visit The Island
Landmark 03 of 07

Mountain Coasters

Experience the thrill of the Smokies in a unique way with the exciting mountain coasters in Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge. These coasters offer a blend of adventure and breathtaking scenery, providing an exhilarating ride through the natural beauty of the mountains. Guests control their speed, making it a fun experience for thrill-seekers or those who prefer a leisurely pace. Each ride on a mountain coaster is not just an activity; it's an unforgettable journey through the heart of the Smoky Mountains, combining excitement with stunning vistas.

Visit Mountain Coasters
Landmark 04 of 07

Titanic Museum

The Titanic Museum in Pigeon Forge offers a captivating historical experience, taking visitors on a journey back to the legendary ocean liner. This unique attraction allows guests to explore full-scale recreations of the ship's interiors and discover hundreds of artifacts from the Titanic. It's an immersive experience that blends history with interactive storytelling, making it a must-visit for history enthusiasts and families alike. The Titanic Museum is more than just an exhibit; it's a poignant tribute to a timeless story, inviting guests to step into history.

Visit Titanic Museum
Landmark 05 of 07

Gravity Park

Gravity Park in Gatlinburg is the ultimate destination for those seeking excitement and fun. Featuring the tallest and fastest zip line in the Smokies and a range of adrenaline-pumping activities like the Skyscraper and the Slingshot, this park is a haven for thrill-seekers. Whether it's racing through the air or experiencing the exhilaration of free-fall, Gravity Park offers unforgettable adventures. It's more than just an amusement park; it's an exhilarating experience that pushes the limits of fun and adventure.

Visit Gravity Park
Landmark 06 of 07

Alcatraz Crime Museum

At the Alcatraz East Crime Museum in Pigeon Forge, visitors embark on an intriguing journey through America's criminal history. This unique museum offers an immersive exploration of crime, punishment, and forensic science, featuring artifacts and stories from notorious criminals to law enforcement heroes. It's an educational and eye-opening experience, providing a deep dive into the complexities of the American justice system. Alcatraz East isn't just a museum; it's a captivating adventure into the darker side of history, inviting curiosity and understanding.

Visit Alcatraz Crime Museum
Landmark 07 of 07

Ziplines

Ziplining in the Smoky Mountains offers an exhilarating way to experience the area's natural beauty from a thrilling new perspective. Glide through the lush forests and over rolling hills, feeling the rush of the wind and the breathtaking views. These zipline adventures cater to everyone, from adrenaline junkies to families looking for an unforgettable experience. It's more than just a ride; it's an opportunity to create lasting memories while soaring high above some of the most picturesque landscapes in Gatlinburg and Pigeon Forge.

Visit Ziplines

Did you know?

Pigeon Forge fun facts

Zorbing Park

Pigeon Forge is home to the only zorbing park in the entire United States

Tourism Hot Spot

Pigeon Forge sees over 11 million visitors a year, which is more than double the number of permanent residents! Only about 6,500 people call Pigeon Forge home

Car Enthusiast

Pigeon Forge is a car enthusiast hub, hosting over 15 annual car events per year

Ripken Baseball Experience

Pigeon Forge is home to The Ripken Baseball Experience, which hosts more than 1,000 teams annually from more than 30 states, traveling from as far away as California, Alaska and overseas. 

Dollywood

In 2022 alone, Dollywood was recognized as Tripadvisor's #1 Theme Park in the United States and won Golden Ticket Awards for Best Kids' Area, Best Christmas Event and Best Guest Experience. Dollywood was also the FIRST theme park to develop a calming room on-site in 2016, for guests who need a break from sensory overload.

Music Mecca

Pigeon Forge is a hotbed of musical talent, and the town hosts numerous music festivals throughout the year. Visitors can enjoy everything from bluegrass to country and rock 'n' roll.

Outlets

Pigeon Forge is home to several large outlet malls and shopping centers, making it a popular destination for shoppers. Visitors can find everything from designer clothing to unique souvenirs.
