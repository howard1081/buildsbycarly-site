Area guide

Gatlinburg

Gatlinburg is a charming mountain town that serves as a gateway to the Great Smoky Mountains National Park. Known for its scenic beauty, outdoor adventures, and rich Appalachian culture, Gatlinburg offers a unique blend of natural attractions and entertainment. From thrilling mountain coasters to tranquil nature walks, it's a destination that promises memorable experiences for every visitor.

Landmarks and attractions

Popular places to see and do in and around Gatlinburg.

Ober Mountain

Ober Mountain in Gatlinburg, Tennessee, offers a unique blend of activities, attracting visitors in every season. With its enchanting winter ski slopes and lively summer trails, this destination is a magnet for those seeking both adventure and relaxation. The renowned aerial tramway and scenic hikes at Ober Mountain create experiences that guests cherish, ensuring a consistent flow of visitors. Discover the captivating appeal of Ober Mountain, a place where every season brings new reasons for guests to visit and revisit.

Visit Ober Mountain

Space Needle

Gatlinburg's Space Needle stands as a beacon of excitement in the heart of the city. This iconic landmark offers breathtaking 360-degree views of the Smoky Mountains and downtown Gatlinburg, captivating visitors of all ages. Imagine a property nearby, where guests can step out to enjoy this and other local attractions like the bustling streets, unique shops, and vibrant cultural experiences. The Space Needle, an unmissable attraction, adds a special allure to the area, drawing visitors year-round for unforgettable moments.

Visit Space Needle

Sky Park

Skypark presents a world of adventure and natural splendor, a perfect draw for those who love the outdoors. This destination thrills visitors with its unique blend of outdoor activities and breathtaking scenery. Close to properties in this area, guests can immerse themselves in experiences like zip-lining, hiking, or simply enjoying panoramic views of the surrounding landscape. Skypark is not just a park; it's a year-round destination for families, thrill-seekers, and nature lovers, making every visit memorable.

Anakeesta

Anakeesta offers a unique mountain experience in Gatlinburg, blending natural beauty with enchanting attractions. Here, guests can wander through the treetop canopy walk, relax in beautifully landscaped gardens, or seek thrills on the mountain coaster. This serene yet exciting destination, nestled in the heart of the Smokies, promises unforgettable moments for every visitor. Climb Works

Climb Works in Gatlinburg offers an exhilarating adventure for those seeking to connect with nature and experience an adrenaline rush. From challenging mountain biking trails to breathtaking zip lines that soar above the treetops, this destination provides an unforgettable outdoor experience. Guests can explore the natural beauty of the Smokies in a thrilling new way, making every visit unique. Ripley's Aquarium

Ripley's Aquarium in Gatlinburg is an underwater world of wonder, captivating visitors with its exotic marine life and interactive experiences. Imagine strolling through a tunnel surrounded by shimmering fish and graceful sharks, creating memories that last a lifetime. This family-friendly attraction, with its educational exhibits and immersive encounters, draws people of all ages. Ripley's Aquarium isn't just an attraction; it's a journey into the depths of the ocean, right in the heart of Gatlinburg.

Gatlinburg fun facts

Ripley's Aquarium

There Are More Fish in Ripley’s Aquarium than People Who Live in Gatlinburg.  The city only has about 4,000 permanent residents. Meanwhile, Ripley’s Aquarium is home to over 10,000 sea creatures.

Wedding Destination

Gatlinburg is the 2nd Most Popular Wedding Destination in the U.S.  The city hosts more weddings than any other location in America, with the exception of Las Vegas.

Ski Resort

Gatlinburg is Home to Tennessee’s Only Ski Resort.  Gatlinburg, TN is the only place in the state where you can go skiing, snowboarding, and snow tubing

Tourism Destination

Gatlinburg receives over 11 million visitors per year

A Bit of History

Gatlinburg’s very first log cabin is still standing!  Although the Cabin is no longer at its original location, it is still intact and open for viewing at the Gatlinburg Welcome Center (520 Parkway).

Pancake House

Gatlinburg is Home to Tennessee’s First Pancake House.  In 1960, the Pancake Pantry opened its doors in Gatlinburg and this legendary restaurant is still in operation on the Parkway.

Moonshine

Moonshine Was Legalized in Gatlinburg in 2009.  The unaged whiskey was known as “moonshine” because it was often made by the light of the moon, in order to avoid the long arm of the law.   Back in the day, farmers would turn their extra corn into whiskey so they could make some extra cash to support their families. 

Sky Bridge

The Gatlinburg SkyBridge is the longest pedestrian cable bridge in North America.
