Welcome to

Lily

The Lily model was named after the speckled wood-lily (Clintonia umbellulata), native to the Great Smoky Mountains. This 1 bed, 1 bath A-frame includes 330 sq ft of living space with a 16’6” x 20’ footprint.
Its bright, open layout makes the most of every inch—ideal for cozy escapes and mountain getaways.
Outdoor living features a covered 8’ x 16’6” rear deck and 4’ x 16’6” front porch, totaling 198 sq ft.

FOUNDATION: BLOCK

Built low to the ground on a traditional block foundation, this version offers a classic cabin profile and a more affordable entry point. It's perfect for flatter lots and budget-conscious buyers. Check out the video and photo gallery to explore this layout up close.

FOUNDATION: TREEHOUSE

Elevated on piers, this “treehouse” version creates a dramatic A-frame look and works beautifully on sloped terrain. It also includes additional site work, reflected in the price. Watch the tour and browse the photos to experience the view from up high.

PROPERTY FEATURES

Finishes & Flexibility

Whether you're building a vacation rental or a personal retreat, the Lily model offers flexibility, comfort, and style. Buyers can personalize a wide range of finishes—including flooring, paint colors, cabinetry, lighting, hardware, and exterior siding—to match their vision. Want to add a larger deck, built-in shelves, or other custom touches? We're happy to help bring those ideas to life.

Systems & Comfort

The Lily model includes full residential systems installed by licensed professionals and built to code. A ductless mini split provides energy-efficient heating and cooling, while the electric fireplace adds ambiance. Standard residential electrical systems are included, plus a 220V line to power a hot tub on the deck. Water and sewer connections depend on the location—public utility or private well/septic. Insulation is up to code for year-round efficiency.

Foundation Options

Each Lily model can be built on either a block foundation or our treehouse-style elevated pier foundation. While some buyers choose based on style or budget, others may need elevation for steeper terrain. Both options are structurally sound, built to code, and chosen based on your land’s slope and soil. We'll help you choose the best foundation to suit your needs while ensuring long-term durability.

Land Included

No need to bring your own lot—the land is included in the purchase price. We handle the land search and purchase, presenting you with only carefully vetted properties that meet your goals. You'll have full approval over the final selection before we begin building. From Smoky Mountain views to wooded privacy, we’ll help you choose the best possible setting for your home.

Compact A-Frame

Unlike most tiny homes of its size, the Lily is a true A-frame—an architectural feat that’s rarely achieved on a 330-square-foot footprint. Its sharply pitched roofline and clean, geometric design make it both iconic and efficient. With soaring ceilings and natural light pouring through the window wall, the Lily offers the dramatic charm of a full-size A-frame while preserving the scale and functionality needed for a small-footprint build.

Space-Saving

The Lily’s bathroom door can be built as either a pocket door or a barn-style sliding door—both perfect for small-space living. These options eliminate the need for door swing clearance, giving you more usable room in the living area and bathroom alike. Whether you prefer the modern simplicity of a pocket door or the rustic charm of a barn door, this thoughtful detail makes everyday movement more efficient and stylish.
