About Builds by Carly

Site-Built Tiny Homes in Sevier County, TN — Serving Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge & Beyond

TENNESSEE

BUILT
IN THE SMOKIES

Builds by Carly is a design-forward construction company based in Sevier County, Tennessee, building custom, site-built tiny homes across the Smoky Mountains. Our homes are crafted to perform as short-term rentals, with thoughtful designs that fit naturally into East Tennessee’s mountain terrain — whether you’re building near Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge, Wears Valley, or deeper in the woods.
Every home is site-built — not shipped or prefab — and carefully matched to the land and rental market. From foundation to finish, our team brings a hands-on approach to mountain living and investment performance.

EXPERIENCE MATTERS

WHO
WE ARE

I’m Carly — a land developer, designer, and builder with a deep connection to the Smokies. Long before this became a company, I was buying land, building cabins, and learning what works (and what doesn’t) in short-term rentals across Sevier County.
Now, I lead a team that shares my commitment to detail and local knowledge. I’m personally involved in every build — from choosing the lot to final walkthrough. We don’t outsource big decisions or push cookie-cutter plans. You’ll see my footprint (literally) on-site throughout the process.

LOCAL MATTERS

WHAT
MAKES US DIFFERENT

Most “tiny home builders” in the Smokies sell kits or prefabs. We don’t. At Builds by Carly, every home is built on-site with local crews who understand zoning, weather, views, and code. These are real homes — with real residential systems — designed to look beautiful and last in the mountain environment.
We offer two foundation types: traditional block or our signature elevated treehouse-style piers, ideal for wooded lots and steep terrain. The treehouse foundation is one of our most popular options and a key visual feature that sets our builds apart.
